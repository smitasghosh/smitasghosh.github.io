Dr. Ghosh’s teaching emphasizes the connection between theoretical foundations and practical problem solving in computer science. Across introductory and advanced courses, she aims to help students develop strong computational reasoning, mathematical maturity, and the ability to apply these ideas to real-world problems. She has taught across the computer science curriculum, ranging from foundational lower-division courses in programming and data structures to specialized upper-division courses in data science, machine learning, and data mining.

Her courses balance accessibility with rigor. Students build confidence through clear explanations, examples, visualizations, and structured practice, then progress toward more independent analysis, implementation, and problem solving.
