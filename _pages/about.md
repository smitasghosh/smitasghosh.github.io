Smita Ghosh is an Associate Professor of Computer Science in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at Santa Clara University. She earned her Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Texas at Dallas under the supervision of Dr. Weili Wu.

Her research advances machine learning and algorithmic methods for networked and socially interconnected systems through two complementary directions:

1. **Graph mining, network science, and social network analysis**, with an emphasis on influence maximization, information diffusion, opinion dynamics, evolutionary graph processes, and hypergraph analysis, and
2. **Machine learning for networked and combinatorial systems**, particularly deep reinforcement learning, learning-augmented algorithms, graph-based learning, and scalable network optimization.

Dr. Ghosh’s work develops data-driven and learning-based methods for understanding and optimizing complex networked systems. Her research spans social influence and information diffusion, reinforcement learning for combinatorial optimization, and interdisciplinary AI, with applications in computational social science, digital mental health, behavioral analysis, and large language model-based support systems.
